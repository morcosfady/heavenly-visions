**Full description (4000 characters max):**

Heavenly Visions is a free Sunday School app for children and their servants in Coptic Orthodox churches. Learn, play and grow in faith, every day of the week.

FOR CHILDREN
• Lesson videos for every grade, from Pre K to Grade 12, matched to the Children of Light curriculum
• Daily verse challenge: read it, fill the words, and keep a growing flame streak
• Stars, levels and badges for coming to class, finishing quizzes and playing games
• Build your own avatar and spend stars in the rewards shop
• Games, quizzes and live class games that your servant starts on the big screen
• The Bible in two easy English translations (WEB and KJV)
• Coptic calendar with feasts, fasts and a saint for each day
• Coloring pages and your own gallery
• Bedtime corner with short Bible stories and a night prayer
• Works offline for lessons, quizzes, games and the calendar

FOR SERVANTS
• Lesson planner with a full screen Sunday Mode for the TV
• Attendance with a daily code and clear charts for your class
• Announcements, events and trips for your class or church
• Follow up list for children who missed Sundays
• Game Builder with 11 game types and an AI helper that writes game drafts you review
• Resource library and a monthly report for Abouna

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
No ads. No tracking. No chat. Children never see other children's names or rankings. You can delete your account in the app at any time.
