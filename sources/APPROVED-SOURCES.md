# Ask Lumi: approved sources

Lumi answers ONLY from cards in `lumi/cards.json` that a servant, coordinator or priest has approved in the app (Servants, Lumi cards). This file says where the card facts come from and what we are allowed to use.

## Rules

1. We never copy long text from books or websites that are not public domain. We write short facts in our own words and name the source.
2. Every card has a `source` label and a `ref`. A card marked `verify` has a detail (a date, a number, a tradition) that a reviewer should double check against a trusted source before approving.
3. Nothing is approved until a person approves it. Edits send a card back to "Waiting".
4. Lumi never searches the internet. It cannot use anything that is not an approved card.

## How to approve a source

Write "approved by [name], [date]" in the table row, or tell us and we will update this file.

## Review checklist for a card

- Is every fact correct Coptic Orthodox teaching?
- Is it kind and age appropriate (no scary or graphic detail)?
- Are dates and numbers right (or left out)?
- Is it in our own words, not copied?
- Does the source label match where the fact really comes from?
